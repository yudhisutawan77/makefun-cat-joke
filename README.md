## Entry

> My cat knocked my phone off the table, watched it shatter, then looked at me like I'd dropped it.

97 characters, one line, nothing else.

## Why this line

The joke is built on one specific, instantly recognisable cat behaviour —
knocking things off surfaces — then escalates it (the cat watches the phone
shatter) and lands the twist in the final clause: the cat stares at the owner
as if *the owner* dropped it. Setup, escalation and blame-reversal punchline
in a single sentence, with no padding and no explanation after the punchline.
It is the kind of line a person retells to a friend the same day, which is
exactly what the judge brief rewards.
